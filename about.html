ABOUT THE PAPER THEORY

Art you can hold. Stories you can display.

The Paper Theory is a creative brand based in Shimla, India. We turn favourite themes and personal ideas into physical objects: laminated sticker packs, custom sticker sheets, colourful Light Boxes, magnets and keepsakes.

Browse ready-made designs or choose stickers from the catalogue to build your own sheet. Our A4 colour-changing Light Boxes bring artwork into your space with a remote included.
